03research proposal · future work

Color in real conditions.

I want to build a replicable VR prototype to study how individuals with color vision deficiency read media pieces in which color carries part of the message.

Type
Master’s research proposal
Method
VR prototype, observation in four stages
Participants
Individuals with color vision deficiency
Status
Proposal. Nothing on this page is built yet.
01

The question

Where my thesis stopped.

In my thesis, I checked color with simulators, on a screen. People meet posters, signs and screens in other conditions: at a distance, for a few seconds, under changing light, among other images.

I want to study what happens to color-coded messages in those conditions, with the people who read them.

How do individuals with color vision deficiency perceive, identify, interpret and understand media pieces in which color carries part of the message?

02

The prototype

Recreated communication situations, the same for every participant.

I want to recreate communication situations in VR and place media pieces inside them. Every participant meets the same piece under the same conditions. Other researchers could run the same setup.

Four variables

Distance

How far the piece is from the viewer.

Competing stimuli

Other images, signs and movement in the scene.

Exposure time

How long the piece stays in view.

Lighting

The light level in the scene.

03

Four stages of observation

From seeing the piece to understanding it.

I want to observe each reading in four stages. A message can break at any of them.

  1. 1

    Perception

    Does the person notice the piece and its colored elements?

  2. 2

    Identification

    Can they tell the colored elements apart and name what they are?

  3. 3

    Interpretation

    What do they think the colors mean?

  4. 4

    Comprehension

    Do they understand the message the piece was made to carry?

04

Study protocol

What the study would test, how, and with whom.

I want to test whether individuals with color vision deficiency read a color-coded media piece in the same way across four viewing conditions. The comparison is within each participant: the same piece, shown under different conditions.

Question

Does the reading of a color-coded piece change with distance, competing stimuli, exposure time and lighting?

Design

Within-subject. Each participant sees the same piece in every condition. The order of conditions is randomized.

Measures

Four stages: perception, identification, interpretation and comprehension, each scored against a fixed criterion.

Outcome

The stage where each reading breaks, by condition. Comparison of breaks across conditions and participants.

Conditions

Participants

The study has not started. No data has been collected.

05

First step

A minimal test scene.

Before the master’s, I plan a minimal scene in A-Frame or Unity: one of my pieces placed at a distance in a virtual room. I will document it as an initial exploration of the setup.
